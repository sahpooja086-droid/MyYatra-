# MyYatra – AI-Powered Personalized Nepal Travel Discovery Platform

## About the Project

**MyYatra** is an AI-powered personalized travel discovery mobile application designed to help domestic and international travellers explore Nepal in a smarter, easier, and more meaningful way.

The application focuses on understanding individual travel interests, preferences, and previous travel experiences to recommend suitable destinations across Nepal. Unlike traditional travel applications that mainly display popular tourist destinations, MyYatra aims to provide personalized recommendations based on each user's unique travel profile.

Through features such as **AI-Generated Journey DNA**, **Exploration Gap Analysis**, and **Personalized District Recommendations**, MyYatra helps travellers discover new experiences, explore less-visited destinations, and track their travel progress across Nepal's 77 districts.

The project aims to promote personalized travel experiences while encouraging travellers to explore Nepal's natural beauty, cultural heritage, adventure destinations, and local attractions.

## Key Features

### 1. AI-Generated Journey DNA
Creates a personalized travel profile based on users' interests, preferences, and recorded travel experiences. It helps the application understand whether users prefer nature, adventure, culture, history, food, photography, or other travel activities.

### 2. AI-Based Exploration Gap Analysis
Analyses users' recorded travel history to identify travel categories and experiences they have explored less frequently. It encourages users to discover new types of destinations and activities.

### 3. Personalized District Recommendations
Recommends suitable districts and destinations across Nepal based on Journey DNA, exploration gaps, and user preferences. The system also aims to explain why a particular destination is recommended.

### 4. 77 Districts Travel Tracking
Allows travellers to record the districts they have visited and monitor their overall exploration progress across Nepal.

### 5. Travel History Management
Helps users record, organize, and manage their previous trips and travel experiences.

### 6. Destination Discovery
Provides information about destinations, tourist attractions, cultural sites, and activities available in different districts of Nepal.

### 7. Favourites
Allows users to save interesting destinations for future exploration.

### 8. User Profile and Preferences
Enables users to manage their profiles, travel interests, and preferences to improve personalized recommendations.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a user-friendly mobile application for personalized travel discovery in Nepal.
- Generate individual travel profiles through Journey DNA.
- Identify exploration opportunities using travel history and preferences.
- Provide personalized district and destination recommendations.
- Allow travellers to track visited districts and manage travel experiences.
- Encourage users to explore Nepal's diverse natural, cultural, and adventure destinations.
